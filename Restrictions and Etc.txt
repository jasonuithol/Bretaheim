[Food]

Food/Rations is represented by barley.  Many places around Britannia will be growing crops, this will always be barley.  Harvesting it from other peoples gardens will affect Karma.

Karma will be listed as a "skill", like STR/DEX etc.

Everyone will "eat" once a day from their inventory 1 barley.  If that is not present, then they lose 30% health.
