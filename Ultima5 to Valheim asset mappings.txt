[Party Status effects]

When donning the crown, for example, the whole party will get status effects.  Since there's no such thing
as a party in Valheim, just give each individual the mapped valheim status effect.

Everyone must be within 1 ultima tile (32 metres was previously discussed as the scaling factor) to receive this benefit.

To get the effect active, "equip" the item (like you would the wishbone, for example)
